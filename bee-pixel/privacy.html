Bee Pixel · Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026 · Türkçe

This policy explains how Bee Pixel, a mobile game developed by saydax (“the App”), handles your personal data.

1. Data we collect

This version of the App does not collect any personal data, does not send data to any server and does not share data with third parties. No account, name, email, location, contacts, photos or device identifiers are requested.

2. Data stored on your device

Your game progress (unlocked levels, stars, coins, lives, boosters) and settings (sound, music, vibration, language) are stored only in the App’s local storage on your device. They never leave your device and are deleted when you uninstall the App.

3. Permissions

  • Vibrate: for in-game haptic feedback. Can be turned off in Settings.
  • Notifications: local reminders created on your device (lives refilled, daily reward, event ending). No server is used; turn them off in Settings › Notifications.

3a. Error log and backup code

The App keeps crash and freeze details (time, device model, error text) only in a file on your device. It is never sent anywhere unless you tap Settings › “Share error log”. “Back up” turns your progress into a text code that only you share.

The App does not require an internet connection.

4. Ads and purchases

This version contains no real ad network and no in-app purchases. If they are added, this policy will be updated before release and will clearly state what data is processed and why.

5. Children

The App does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13; it does not collect personal data from any user.

6. Your rights

Since no personal data is processed, there is no server-side record to delete or export. You can delete local game data by uninstalling the App or via Android Settings › Apps › Bee Pixel › Storage › Clear data.

8. Changes

When this policy changes, the date above is updated. Significant changes are announced with an App update.
